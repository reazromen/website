# Kosovo–Serbia Armed Incidents and Organized Violence

Kosovo and Serbia remain locked in a dispute over sovereignty and recognition, but political tension and armed conflict are not interchangeable categories. Since 2000, the security picture has included post-war stabilization, intercommunal unrest, attacks on security personnel, and episodic armed incidents in northern Kosovo.

## Timeline and actors

The March 2004 unrest, later attacks and the September 2023 Banjska incident differ in participants, scale and political context. NATO’s Kosovo Force (KFOR), EU-mediated dialogue and Kosovo and Serbian security institutions all shape the environment. These events need to be described individually instead of treating every year as a continuous war.

## Scope and classification

This entry focuses on documented organized violence. A separate political timeline can explain the status dispute, but a qualifying armed-conflict claim needs event-level evidence and a clear classification.
