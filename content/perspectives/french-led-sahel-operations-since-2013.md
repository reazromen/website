# French-Led Sahel Operations Since 2013

French military operations in the Sahel included Operation Serval in Mali in 2013 and the later regional Operation Barkhane. These deployments sought to support partner governments and combat jihadist armed groups, while operating amid rebellions, local conflicts, coups and shifting public attitudes toward foreign forces.

## Timeline and actors

The campaign’s geography and partnerships changed over time. Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger each had distinct governments, armed actors and political transitions; a regional operational label should not erase those differences. The French withdrawal from several Sahel countries also changed the security landscape and the role of other external partners.

## Scope and classification

This is an intervention cross-index linked to the national conflict essays. It should not be counted as a separate war without a defined conflict dyad. Assessments of effectiveness must distinguish territorial control, civilian harm, insurgent adaptation, local governance and public legitimacy.
