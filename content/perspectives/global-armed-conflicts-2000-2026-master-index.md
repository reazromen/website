# Global Armed Conflicts, 2000–2026: Master Archive and Research Index

**Browse the regional catalogue:** [Global Armed Conflicts, 2000–2026: Catalogue](/posts/global-armed-conflicts-2000-2026.html).


This is the working master index for a global archive of armed conflict from 1 January 2000 through 15 October 2026. Its purpose is to organize the research programme, expose gaps, and prevent the archive from becoming a collection of disconnected essays about only the most familiar wars.

**This is a coverage register, not a claim that every entry is a separate war or that every event has already been verified.** Several entries are theatres, phases, insurgencies, cross-border campaigns, or overlapping labels for violence that may belong under one parent conflict. They must be reconciled against the underlying datasets before the archive describes its coverage as exhaustive.

## How the archive defines its scope

The archive uses three complementary layers:

1. **State-based armed conflict:** organized fighting in which a state government is one party.
2. **Non-state armed conflict:** organized fighting between non-state armed groups.
3. **One-sided violence against civilians:** deliberate violence by organized actors against civilians. These campaigns matter to the history of organized violence, but they are not automatically separate wars.

The principal statistical backbone is the Uppsala Conflict Data Program (UCDP). Its 2026 release of the UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset covers 1946–2025; its non-state conflict and one-sided violence datasets cover 1989–2025. The UCDP Candidate Events Dataset supplies preliminary event-level reporting for 2026. Candidate events are provisional and can be revised; they should not be presented as final annual totals. Dataset definitions also differ from the ordinary meaning of “war,” so the archive will preserve both the dataset classification and the editorial explanation.

**Coverage window:** 2000–2025 for annual dataset cross-checks, plus a clearly labelled provisional 2026 update through 15 October 2026.

## Reading the status labels

- **Published** means an essay has already been added to this site’s source repository. Live deployment must still be checked independently.
- **Planned** means it is on the research queue, not that a complete article exists.
- **Cross-check** means the entry may overlap with another conflict or may not meet the formal armed-conflict threshold for the entire period.

## Required audit before calling the archive exhaustive

The manual list above is a starting register of major named conflicts and research leads. The final audit must also inspect the complete UCDP conflict-year and dyad-year records for every year from 2000 through 2025, the non-state conflict dataset, and the one-sided violence dataset. That audit may add local or short-lived conflicts not widely described as “wars.” It must also remove duplicates, preserve alternate names, and mark entries that do not meet the archive’s inclusion threshold.

For 2026, the editor must use the latest available UCDP Candidate Events release and label the coverage date and provisional status. It must not silently combine candidate events with final annual data as though they were equivalent.

### Editorial rule

Every standalone essay should identify the dates, geography, parties, conflict classification, civilian consequences, major turning points, and what remains disputed or uncertain. Separate verified facts from estimates and interpretations. Do not equate casualty counts across datasets without explaining their definitions, and do not turn a list of armed incidents into a claim of continuous war.

The archive is complete only when the dataset audit is recorded, duplicate and borderline cases are explained, and every eligible conflict has either a published essay or an explicit research status. This page is the master register for that process.
