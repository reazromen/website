# Korean Peninsula Armed Incidents

The Korean Peninsula has experienced recurring military incidents without a continuous resumption of the Korean War. The 1953 armistice halted large-scale fighting but did not produce a peace treaty. Since 2000, the security record has included naval clashes, artillery exchanges, border incidents, missile tests and military crises with differing levels of lethality.

## Timeline and actors

Major incidents such as the 2002 naval clash, the 2010 sinking of the Cheonan and the shelling of Yeonpyeong, and later periods of heightened missile and military activity should be dated and classified individually. Not every missile test or threat is armed violence; coercive signalling must be separated from lethal exchanges.

## Scope and classification

This entry is a chronology of discrete incidents and escalation risks, not a claim of an uninterrupted war from 2000 to 2026. Event-level records should identify the location, weapons used, fatalities, official claims and independent findings. Broader diplomatic tension belongs in context, not in the event count.
