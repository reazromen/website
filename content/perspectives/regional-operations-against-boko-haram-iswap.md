# Regional Operations Against Boko Haram and ISWAP

The Boko Haram insurgency spread from northeastern Nigeria into the Lake Chad Basin, affecting Cameroon, Chad and Niger. Regional responses included national military operations and the Multinational Joint Task Force. The Islamic State West Africa Province emerged from organizational divisions within the insurgent movement, and the relationship between factions has shifted over time.

## Timeline and actors

Cross-border cooperation reflects the reality that armed groups use frontier regions and that civilians move across the same spaces. Yet each state’s operations, local armed actors and civilian-protection challenges differ. Military pressure can disrupt territorial control while also displacing communities and changing insurgent tactics.

## Scope and classification

This entry records the regional intervention layer alongside national and subregional conflict essays. It should not replace the Boko Haram/ISWAP conflict record or imply that all partner forces operate under one unified command. Claims about deaths, territorial control and group affiliations require dated corroboration.
