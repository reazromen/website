# US-Led Coalition Operations in Afghanistan, 2001–2021

US-led coalition operations in Afghanistan began in October 2001 and continued through changing missions until the international military withdrawal in 2021. The intervention initially targeted al-Qaeda and the Taliban government that hosted its leadership. It then became connected to a prolonged insurgency, the establishment and support of Afghan state institutions, and successive changes in international strategy.

## From invasion to support mission

The opening campaign rapidly removed the Taliban government from Kabul, but it did not end the conflict. Taliban forces and other armed groups reorganized, and the war expanded across multiple provinces. International forces conducted combat operations while also training and advising Afghan security institutions. NATO’s International Security Assistance Force (ISAF) and later Resolute Support had different mandates and structures; they should not be treated as one unchanged mission.

Coalition participation also varied. Countries contributed troops, air support, training or other capabilities under different national rules. Casualty and operation figures therefore depend on the period and the force being counted. An accurate article should state whether a figure covers US forces, all international forces or Afghan partners.

## The 2021 withdrawal

The final withdrawal and the collapse of the Afghan government in August 2021 marked a major transition. They did not erase the preceding two decades or establish that all forms of violence in Afghanistan had ended. The Taliban’s return to power changed the parties and nature of subsequent violence, which should be assessed separately from the international intervention period.

## Archive treatment

This page documents the coalition layer within the Afghanistan conflict. It should be linked to the main Afghanistan essay and not counted as a second war solely because foreign troops participated. Direct combat, training, support and civilian harm need distinct descriptions, dates and sources.
