# The Global War on Terror After 2001

The “Global War on Terror” is a political and military umbrella that followed the attacks of 11 September 2001. It brought together operations with different legal bases, adversaries, partners and timelines, including the invasion of Afghanistan, later operations in Iraq, counterterrorism campaigns in parts of Africa and Asia, and remote-strike programmes.

## Timeline and actors

The umbrella connects policies and institutions, but it is not one conflict with one battlefield or a single opposing organization. Al-Qaeda, the Islamic State and local affiliates have had distinct leadership and objectives; host governments and international partners have also differed by theatre. Operations described as counterterrorism can range from conventional combat to intelligence cooperation and arrests.

## Scope and classification

This archive entry is a strategic overview. Casualties and legal assessments must be assigned to individual operations and theatres. The label “war on terror” should not be used to double-count Afghanistan, Iraq, Somalia, Yemen or other conflicts already documented as primary entries.
