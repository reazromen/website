# Foreign Military Support in Ukraine

Since Russia’s full-scale invasion in February 2022, Ukraine has received extensive military, financial and intelligence support from a coalition of partner states. Support has included weapons, ammunition, air defence, training, logistics and economic assistance. Russia has also received external support, including material and weapons relationships documented by governments and international monitoring bodies.

## Timeline and actors

Support does not automatically make every donor a direct belligerent, and the legal characterization of involvement depends on facts and applicable law. The scale, type and restrictions on support have changed over time. These details matter to understanding operational capacity, escalation risks and the political debate over aid.

## Scope and classification

This is an intervention and assistance index linked to the main Russo-Ukrainian war record. It should not be counted as a separate war from Russia’s invasion. Claims about specific transfers, battlefield use or third-party participation should cite dated official statements and independent verification.
