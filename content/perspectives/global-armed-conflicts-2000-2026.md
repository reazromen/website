# Global Armed Conflicts, 2000–2026: Catalogue

This catalogue is the navigation layer for the [master research index](/posts/global-armed-conflicts-2000-2026-master-index.html). It organizes the archive by region and theatre. The catalogue is not a claim that every listed theatre is a separate war, nor that all entries have completed source-level dataset reconciliation.

## How to use this catalogue

- **Read an essay** opens a published article.
- **Research index** identifies an entry that still needs a dedicated essay or a scope decision.
- Some entries are phases of a larger conflict, external interventions, cross-border campaigns or one-sided violence. Those labels should not be counted as independent wars without checking definitions and overlap.
- The archive uses UCDP/PRIO as a core reference framework. Annual datasets currently end in 2025; 2026 event reporting is provisional and should be labeled accordingly.

## Americas and the Caribbean

The research queue includes Colombia’s wider armed conflict and specific armed organizations, organized armed violence in Mexico and parts of Latin America, Haiti’s gang warfare, Ecuador, Brazil and cross-border theatres. High homicide, political instability or organized crime alone does not automatically establish a war; each record requires an explicit definition and evidence base.

## International interventions and cross-border campaigns

Intervention and campaign pages are cross-indexes, not extra wars to add mechanically to a conflict count. They cover the post-2001 Global War on Terror, coalition operations in Afghanistan, Operation Inherent Resolve, Libya, the Sahel, Somalia, the Central African Republic, Lake Chad, Yemen, Ukraine, transnational ISIS affiliates, remote-strike campaigns and private military companies.

## Method and limitations

The catalogue’s links identify where an essay exists; they do not substitute for a dataset audit. Before the archive claims exhaustive coverage, each entry must be compared with UCDP/PRIO state-based, dyadic, non-state and one-sided-violence records, and 2026 reporting must remain provisional. The [master index](/posts/global-armed-conflicts-2000-2026-master-index.html) records scope and research status.
