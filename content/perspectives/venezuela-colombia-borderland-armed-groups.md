# Venezuela–Colombia Borderland Armed Groups

The Venezuela–Colombia border has been affected by Colombian armed organizations, dissident factions, the ELN, local criminal groups and security forces on both sides. Geography, smuggling routes, migration, uneven state presence and political tensions have shaped the borderland, but these factors should not be collapsed into one transnational war.

## Timeline and actors

The 2016 Colombian peace agreement altered the armed landscape without ending violence in all regions. Some factions rejected or later left the peace process, while the ELN maintained a separate organization and campaign. Reports of cross-border presence and alleged state relationships require careful attribution; a group operating in a country does not automatically mean that country’s government directs it.

## Scope and classification

This entry links related Colombian and Venezuelan theatres. Each incident should name the organization involved, the location, the year and the evidence for any state role. Displacement and criminal economies are relevant context but are not, by themselves, proof of a specific armed-conflict dyad.
