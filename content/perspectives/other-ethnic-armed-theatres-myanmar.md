# Other Ethnic Armed Theatres in Myanmar

Myanmar’s ethnic armed organizations have distinct histories, political programmes, territorial bases and relationships with the military authorities. Beyond the Kachin, Karen, Karenni and Rakhine theatres, Shan, Chin and other areas have experienced different patterns of fighting, ceasefire arrangements and resistance activity. Treating them as one undifferentiated insurgency hides local causes and alliances.

## Timeline and actors

The February 2021 coup transformed the national conflict by connecting longstanding ethnic armed organizations with newer People’s Defence Forces and other resistance networks. Yet cooperation varies, and not every organization shares a single command or political objective. Local ceasefires, territorial competition and cross-border dynamics can differ substantially between states and townships.

## Scope and classification

This entry is an index for identifying additional actor-specific essays, not a substitute for them. Candidate theatres should be added only after checking named organizations, dates, geographic scope and overlap with the nationwide post-coup war. The archive should avoid treating an ethnic identity as if it were itself an armed actor.
