# China–India Border Clashes, Including the 2020 Galwan Incident

China and India have a long-running boundary dispute along a contested frontier. Most confrontations have involved patrols, infrastructure, face-offs and disengagement negotiations rather than sustained war. The June 2020 Galwan Valley clash was a major lethal episode and triggered a prolonged military stand-off in eastern Ladakh.

## Timeline and actors

The wider dispute spans several sectors, including western Ladakh and the eastern sector around Arunachal Pradesh. Agreements on border management have not resolved the boundary itself. Military deployments and road construction can change local risk, while negotiations may reduce immediate tensions without settling competing territorial claims.

## Scope and classification

This essay separates the lethal Galwan event from the larger border dispute and from other incidents. A robust archive records confirmed fatalities and the source of each claim, and does not label every stand-off or diplomatic confrontation as an armed conflict. The 2020 event should be placed in its own dated episode within the broader border-security timeline.
