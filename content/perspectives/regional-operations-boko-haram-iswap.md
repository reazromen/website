# Regional Operations Against Boko Haram and ISWAP in the Lake Chad Basin

The Boko Haram insurgency began in northeastern Nigeria and expanded across the Lake Chad Basin, affecting Cameroon, Chad and Niger. As armed groups crossed borders and attacked civilians and security forces, the affected states developed bilateral operations and regional cooperation, including the Multinational Joint Task Force (MNJTF).

## A multi-country conflict

Boko Haram has fragmented over time. One faction became associated with the Islamic State and is widely known as Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP). The groups have competed, attacked civilians and security forces, and adapted to military pressure. Their territorial influence and operating methods differ by period and locality, so the term “Boko Haram” should not be used as a catch-all for every armed incident in the region.

The MNJTF brings together forces from countries around Lake Chad and has operated alongside national military campaigns. Cooperation has varied in intensity, command arrangements and access to resources. A regional operation can affect multiple national theatres without making the participating states parties to one identical conflict in every location.

## Civilian harm and data

The conflict has involved killings, abductions, forced recruitment, displacement and restrictions on livelihoods. The attribution of individual attacks can be difficult, particularly where groups use similar tactics or claim responsibility strategically. The archive should distinguish confirmed events from claims, and avoid combining casualties from national forces, non-state groups and one-sided violence without explaining the categories.

## Archive treatment

This page documents the regional response as an intervention layer. It should be linked to the main Lake Chad Basin conflict entry and distinguish the MNJTF from national operations and international assistance. The same attack should not be counted once under each country’s campaign unless the record genuinely represents separate events.
