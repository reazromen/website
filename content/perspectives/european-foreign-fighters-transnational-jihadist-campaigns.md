# European Foreign Fighters and Transnational Jihadist Campaigns

Foreign fighters from Europe joined armed organizations in Syria, Iraq and other theatres during the 2000s and especially the territorial expansion of the Islamic State after 2013. Their travel, recruitment and return created security, prosecution, rehabilitation and citizenship questions across European states.

## Timeline and actors

The term “foreign fighter” describes a person’s relationship to a conflict, not a standalone conflict actor or a single campaign. Individuals joined different organizations, served in different roles and travelled at different times. Some participated in hostilities; others supported logistics, propaganda or recruitment. National responses have included criminal investigations, border controls, repatriation debates and deradicalization programmes.

## Scope and classification

This archive entry is a transnational research lens. It should not be counted as a separate war or used to imply that all foreign travellers were combatants. Specific allegations require court records or independently corroborated reporting, and each person’s role must be distinguished from group-level claims.
