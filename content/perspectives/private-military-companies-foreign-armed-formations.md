# Private Military Companies and Foreign Armed Formations

Private military and security companies, foreign volunteer formations and state-linked contractors have participated in several contemporary conflict theatres. Their legal status, command relationships and missions vary widely. Some provide logistics or site security; others have reportedly taken direct combat roles or operated alongside state forces.

## Timeline and actors

The term “mercenary” has a specific legal meaning and should not be used as a synonym for every contractor or foreign volunteer. A careful entry distinguishes corporate structure, recruitment, financing, command, battlefield role and relationship with a state. Public evidence may be incomplete, and claims about covert control require corroboration.

## Scope and classification

This is an actor and intervention cross-index rather than a separate war count. Each deployment should be tied to a named theatre and time period. The archive should differentiate verified facts from allegations and avoid treating all foreign formations as a single organization.
