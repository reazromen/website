# Transnational Islamic State Campaigns and Affiliates

The Islamic State developed from a territorial organization in Iraq and Syria into a network of affiliates and aligned groups operating in several regions. Affiliates have had varying degrees of operational autonomy, local agendas and links to the central organization. The loss of territorial control in Iraq and Syria did not end all activity associated with the brand.

## Timeline and actors

The Sahel, Lake Chad Basin, Somalia, Afghanistan and other theatres have seen different organizations claim affiliation or recognition. Their local recruitment, opponents, tactics and territorial ambitions vary. A shared name or pledge of allegiance is not enough to prove a single command structure or identical strategy.

## Scope and classification

This entry is a transnational index. Each affiliate should be documented through a separate local conflict record where evidence supports it, with clear distinctions among confirmed organizational links, self-proclaimed affiliation and propaganda claims. The umbrella should not inflate the count of wars or replace country-level analysis.
