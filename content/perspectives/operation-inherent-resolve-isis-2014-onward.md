# Operation Inherent Resolve Against the Islamic State

Operation Inherent Resolve is the US-led coalition campaign launched in 2014 against the Islamic State in Iraq and Syria. It combined air strikes, advising and assistance, intelligence, logistics and partner-force support. The campaign helped defeat the group’s territorial control in Iraq and Syria, while ISIS networks and attacks continued after the loss of its territorial “caliphate.”

## Timeline and actors

The operation crossed two national theatres with different governments, armed actors and legal arrangements. In Iraq, coalition support worked with Iraqi and Kurdish forces; in Syria, the coalition partnered principally with the Syrian Democratic Forces in the anti-ISIS campaign. These relationships changed over time and coexisted with other military interventions and local conflicts.

## Scope and classification

This is an intervention record linked to the Iraq and Syria essays. It should not be counted as an independent war apart from the underlying conflicts. Event claims require dated coalition records and independent reporting, particularly when assessing civilian casualties or attributing a strike.
