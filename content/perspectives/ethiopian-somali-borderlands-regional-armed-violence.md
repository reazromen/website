# Ethiopian–Somali Borderlands and Regional Armed Violence

The Ethiopia–Somalia borderlands have been shaped by state security campaigns, Somali regional politics, cross-border militant movement and competition over local authority. These dynamics overlap with the wider Somali conflict and Ethiopia’s internal armed conflicts, but they do not form one uninterrupted war with a single set of parties.

## Timeline and actors

The Ogaden insurgency led by the Ogaden National Liberation Front followed a distinct trajectory, including a 2018 peace agreement. Separately, Ethiopia has participated in operations in Somalia against al-Shabaab, while federal and regional authorities in both countries have faced shifting security priorities. Cross-border activity can involve local militias, state forces and transnational armed groups whose goals differ.

This entry is a regional map of related theatres.
