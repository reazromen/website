# US-Led Coalition Operations in Afghanistan

The US-led intervention in Afghanistan began in October 2001 and evolved from the overthrow of the Taliban government into a long international military presence supporting Afghan state institutions and fighting Taliban and other armed groups. Coalition roles, troop levels and rules of engagement changed over two decades, before the international withdrawal and Taliban takeover in 2021.

## Timeline and actors

This military campaign is inseparable from the wider Afghan conflict but is analytically distinct as an intervention layer. The campaign involved US and allied forces, Afghan security institutions, the Taliban, al-Qaeda and other actors whose relationships changed over time. Civilian harm, detention, air operations, reconstruction and the 2021 evacuation each require their own evidence and chronology.

This entry should be read alongside the main Afghanistan war essay, not counted as a separate independent war. The archive separates intervention decisions and coalition operations from the overall conflict trajectory so that external involvement is visible without inflating the number of wars.
