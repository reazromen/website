We do not know what this sequence does.

From that sentence, two opposite mistakes can begin.

Mistake one:

therefore it does nothing.

Mistake two:

therefore it must have a hidden purpose we have not discovered yet.

Both convert ignorance into knowledge.

Unknown function does not imply no function.

It also does not imply secret function.

## Evolution does not promise usefulness

Natural selection can preserve beneficial variants.

It can remove strongly harmful variants.

But genomes also change through genetic drift, mutation, recombination, duplication, transposable elements, and demographic history.

A sequence can persist without serving a current adaptive role.

Existence alone is not evidence of purpose.

Evolution is not an engineer keeping only needed components.

## "Purpose" smuggles intention

Biologists often use teleological shorthand.

The heart is "for" pumping blood.

Bird wings are "for" flight.

The shorthand can be convenient if we remember the underlying evolutionary meaning.

Structures were not designed because a future organism needed them.

Variants affecting survival and reproduction changed frequency over generations.

Function is historical and causal.

Purpose in the intentional sense is not required.

## Hidden-function stories are unfalsifiable when vague

Suppose every failed experiment is answered with:

the function exists, but under some unknown condition.

Now no observation can count against the claim.

That is not a useful scientific hypothesis.

A good hidden-function hypothesis should make a prediction.

This sequence affects heat stress.

This RNA regulates one pathway.

This enhancer acts during a developmental window.

Now experiments can fail.

Risk makes the claim scientific.

## Lack of conservation can matter

If a sequence varies rapidly across related species without obvious constraint, that can count against some claims of essential conserved function.

Not all functions require strong sequence conservation.

Different lineages can evolve different mechanisms.

But evolutionary patterns belong in the evidence.

Unknown function should not erase what comparative genomics already tells us.

## Molecular activity can be incidental

A DNA region can be transcribed at low levels.

A protein can bind weakly.

A chromatin mark can appear.

Cells are noisy molecular environments.

Activity does not automatically imply adaptive function.

The stronger claim requires evidence that the activity matters to the system in a biologically relevant way.

This distinction became especially important in debates over functional non-coding DNA.

## Discovery remains possible

None of this should produce cynicism.

Biology repeatedly discovers unexpected functions.

Sequences once ignored become important regulators.

Transposable-element fragments become enhancers.

Non-coding RNAs acquire major roles.

Novel genes emerge from previously non-genic sequence.

The correct attitude is open without being credulous.

## Keep the middle category alive

Science needs a stable place for:

we do not know.

Without that category, every unknown is forced toward either dismissal or mythology.

Unknown function ≠ hidden purpose.

It means exactly what it says:

current evidence has not established the role.

The next step is not to choose a preferred story.

It is to design a measurement that lets biology answer.
