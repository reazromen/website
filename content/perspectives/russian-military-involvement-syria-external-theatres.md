# Russian Military Involvement in Syria and Other External Theatres

Russia’s overseas military activity is best documented as a set of dated interventions connected to local conflicts, rather than as a single global war. Its September 2015 intervention in Syria changed the balance of power through air operations, advisers and support for the Syrian government. Russian deployments and military relationships have also appeared in other theatres, with different legal, political and operational arrangements.

## Timeline and actors

For each theatre, analysts should identify the deployment, its mandate or stated rationale, the local forces supported, the nature of combat activity and the time period. Syria’s war remains the primary conflict record; Russian intervention is an external-involvement layer within it. The same principle applies wherever Russian personnel, contractors or equipment operate in an existing conflict.
