# External Military Support in Yemen

Yemen’s war has involved direct fighting among Yemeni actors and external support from regional states. The Saudi-led coalition intervened in 2015 in support of the internationally recognized government; Iran has been widely reported to provide support to the Houthi movement, while other states have supplied weapons, intelligence or diplomatic backing at different times.

## Timeline and actors

Support relationships vary in type and degree. Arms transfers, training, intelligence, direct air operations and political backing should not be treated as equivalent. Yemeni actors retain their own leadership and local constituencies, and external support does not prove complete command by a foreign sponsor.

## Scope and classification

This entry is a cross-index, not an additional war count. The archive should connect each support relationship to a named recipient, a dated source and a specific theatre. Civilian harm and legal responsibility require separate evidence and should not be inferred solely from the existence of an alliance.
