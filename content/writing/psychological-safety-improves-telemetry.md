Observability is partly a technical system and partly a reporting culture. Metrics cannot capture the engineer who noticed an odd behavior but stayed quiet because it did not yet qualify as a formal incident.

The most useful hserver improvements often started from uncomfortable weak signals: a dashboard that looked wrong, memory that felt too high, backup evidence that was technically fresh but incomplete, or authentication behavior that was confusing.

Psychological safety means those observations can be raised without requiring certainty or blame. Engineering still validates the claim, but curiosity is cheaper than silence.

A healthy DevOps team treats early uncertainty as input to investigation, not as an admission of failure, because weak signals are often the cheapest moment to learn.
