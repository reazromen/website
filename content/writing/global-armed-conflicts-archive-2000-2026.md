# Global Armed Conflicts Archive, 2000–2026

This is the complete working register for the archive's current 115 conflict and theatre entries. It is published as a coverage catalogue while source reconciliation and URL verification continue. **A listing here is not a claim that every entry is a separately published, fully researched article or a confirmed UCDP conflict classification.**

- **5** existing articles were previously live-verified.
- **102** entries are planned for editorial development.
- **8** entries require explicit scope or cross-check review.

Dataset reconciliation for 2000–2025 remains incomplete. Dates, parties, casualty estimates, classification, duplicate/phase relationships, and inclusion decisions must be checked against primary datasets and reliable reporting. No unverified casualty totals are asserted in this catalogue.
