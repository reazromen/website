# Signals of Reality — 200 Article Plan

Editorial constraints:
- English only.
- Long-form popular-science / systems essays, usually 900–1600 words.
- Natural variety: do not reuse one article template. Vary openings, sectioning, pacing, argument shape, examples, and endings.
- Use high-level craft lessons associated with strong science journalism and essay writing without copying any writer's voice.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources. Distinguish established fact, model, interpretation, hypothesis, and speculation.
- No fabricated quotations, experiments, personal experiences, or citations.
- When a technical analogy breaks, say where it breaks.
- Publish no more than 5 new project articles per real calendar day in Asia/Dhaka; do not evade this by backdating. Also retain the site-wide metadata-date limit of 5 posts per date.
- New publication dates reflect the actual release date. Draft dates are provisional and must be reassigned at release.
- New articles belong in content/perspectives/.
- Keep existing site architecture and URLs intact.
